A single pass leaves floating slivers between pads — the sliver predictor (check 9) computes the required width from the actual gap geometry and blocks “job ready” if the configured value leaves slivers near solderable pads. Offset solver (FR5.3)

Centres the copper on the blank by MEASUREMENT: runs pcb2gcode at two trial offsets, reads actual extents from the generated G-code (not the console), measures the gain — on this mirrored back-side board x-offset acts in mirrored X, a positive value moves the toolpath more negative — then solves and verifies with a final run. Takes ~15 s.
